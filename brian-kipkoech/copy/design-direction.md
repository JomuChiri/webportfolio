# Design Direction - Brian Kipkoech

## Visual Goal
Create a premium scientific personal brand website for a molecular biology and biotechnology professional. The page should feel precise, research-oriented, trustworthy, and modern without becoming cold or generic.

## Brand Feeling
- Scientific
- Calm
- Precise
- Credible
- Focused
- Professional

## Color Direction
- Deep laboratory green: primary accent for bioscience, growth, and trust
- Ink charcoal: primary text and strong contrast
- Clean white: main reading surface
- Soft microscope blue: secondary accent for molecular biology and technical clarity
- Warm ivory: subtle page background warmth
- Muted amber: restrained highlight for achievement and calls to action

## Typography Mood
- Headings: strong contemporary serif or high-contrast editorial display type
- Body: clean scientific sans-serif with excellent legibility
- Type feel: precise, composed, and easy to scan
- Avoid playful, startup-like, or overly decorative type choices

## Layout Direction
- Hero-first layout with Brian's supplied portrait clearly visible
- Large name treatment with molecular biology role line
- Research focus section near the top for immediate relevance
- Experience timeline with laboratory highlights separated from leadership experience
- Skills grouped into molecular workflows, laboratory practice, and coordination
- Contact section that feels professional and collaboration-oriented

## UI Style
- Thin borders and controlled shadows
- Clean section bands with generous spacing
- Compact scientific cards for skills and research highlights
- Clear primary and secondary CTA buttons
- Navigation labels should map to anchored sections
- Use restrained rounded corners, no oversized pill-heavy interface

## Imagery Direction
- Use the supplied headshot in the hero section
- Generate favicon from the same image
- Generate and link `assets/og-image.jpg`
- Keep the portrait crisp, visible, and professional on desktop and mobile
- Avoid dark overlays that hide the face

## Interaction Direction
- Subtle hover states on cards and links
- Gentle section reveal or lift is acceptable
- No distracting motion, animated science gimmicks, or decorative clutter

## Copy Treatment
- Use the verified copy package as the source of truth
- Make technical experience readable and structured
- Keep claims careful and factual
- Present the page as a personal brand portfolio, not a plain resume

## Overall Direction
The final site should feel like a polished biotechnology profile for a molecular biology professional who can support research teams with careful laboratory technique, molecular workflows, data interpretation, and dependable academic collaboration.
