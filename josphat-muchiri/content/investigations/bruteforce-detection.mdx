This investigation examines repeated failed login attempts against a target account and shows how an analyst can move from raw log evidence to a clear security recommendation.

## Recommendations

- Review targeted account activity.
- Enforce MFA where possible.
- Block or investigate the source host.
- Tune detections for failed logon thresholds by time window.
