## Overview

A good SOC process keeps the analyst grounded in evidence. The goal is to avoid assumptions, collect relevant facts, and communicate a clear conclusion.

## Process

1. Understand the alert.
2. Identify affected assets and users.
3. Collect logs and supporting evidence.
4. Build a short timeline.
5. Map behavior to known techniques only when evidence supports it.
6. Recommend containment or follow-up.
7. Document lessons learned.
